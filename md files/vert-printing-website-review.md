# Vert Printing Website Review

## Overall Assessment

The live Vert Printing website is much improved and now feels like a credible local printing and branding business rather than a generic template.

The site is clear, easy to understand, and suitable for launch. The main focus from here should be improving visual proof, strengthening trust signals, and tightening a small amount of repeated copy.

## What Is Working Well

### Clear positioning

The homepage quickly explains what Vert Printing does:

> Custom printing and branding, made properly.

The supporting copy clearly covers:

- Clothing
- Mugs
- Stickers
- Labels
- Embroidery
- Laser cutting

Visitors do not need to search around to understand the services available.

### Strong quote form

The quote form asks for useful information upfront, including:

- Product required
- Quantity
- Deadline
- Preferred contact method
- Artwork upload

This should reduce unnecessary back-and-forth and help Vert provide faster, more accurate quotes.

### Good local relevance

The website makes the local service area clear by mentioning Kloof and Durban naturally.

The physical address, phone number, email address, and WhatsApp contact options are also easy to find.

### Credible target market

The copy speaks directly to:

- Businesses
- Schools
- Teams and clubs
- Individuals

This is more effective than using broad, vague messaging.

### Believable value proposition

The “Why choose Vert” section focuses on practical benefits:

- Personal service
- Selecting the correct production method
- Assistance with artwork
- Reliable communication

These claims feel more credible than generic statements such as “best quality” or “lowest prices.”

## Recommended Improvements

### 1. Strengthen the Our Work section

For a printing business, real photographs of completed work will be one of the strongest sales tools on the site.

The gallery should include varied examples such as:

- Close-up embroidery
- Printed shirts and uniforms
- Branded mugs
- Stickers and labels
- Laser-cut products
- Corporate branding jobs

Each image could include a short caption explaining the job, product, or production method.

### 2. Add more testimonials

One testimonial is useful, but it is not enough to provide strong social proof.

Aim for at least three genuine reviews from different customer types:

- A business or corporate client
- A school, club, or sports team
- An individual customer

### 3. Reduce repeated copy near the top

There is some overlap between:

- The main hero section
- The custom quote support section
- The services introduction
- The final quote section

The “Tell us what you need. We’ll help you make it” section could be shortened or removed because the hero and quote form already communicate this clearly.

### 4. Check old indexed pages

Old website content may still appear in search results.

Check that outdated WordPress pages:

- Redirect to the correct new page or section
- Return a proper 404 or 410 if they are no longer needed
- Do not remain accessible as duplicate pages

### 5. Add dedicated service pages later

The one-page structure is suitable for launch, but separate service pages would improve long-term SEO.

Recommended future pages:

- Garment printing in Kloof and Durban
- Embroidery services
- Sticker and label printing
- Mug printing
- Laser cutting and engraving
- Corporate branding

## Recommended Priority Order

1. Improve the project photography.
2. Add more real testimonials.
3. Redirect or remove old indexed URLs.
4. Reduce repeated homepage copy.
5. Add dedicated service pages for SEO.

## Final View

The site is clear, credible, and ready to launch.

It does not need another redesign. The next improvements should focus on:

- Showing real completed work
- Building trust through reviews
- Improving search visibility
- Tracking quote and WhatsApp conversions
